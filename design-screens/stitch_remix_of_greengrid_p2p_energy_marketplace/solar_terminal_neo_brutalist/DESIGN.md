## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-velocity financial telemetries with the unapologetic raw aesthetic of clean-energy infrastructure. Think institutional financial terminal meets radical decentralized energy grid. The personality is definitive, hyper-tactile, high-density, and mechanically precise.

The target audience comprises energy brokers, smart-grid operators, industrial solar prosumers, and clean-tech infrastructure engineers who demand instantaneous data consumption without fluff. The UI evokes urgency, mechanical certainty, and industrial reliability through stark neo-brutalist fundamentals: heavy structural ink lines, raw rectangular surfaces, unyielding grid divisions, and hard 90-degree tactile offsets that react with mechanical clickiness.

## Colors

The palette relies on absolute contrast and stark, industrial energy indicators:

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the foundational canvas, while Card Surfaces leverage an ultra-subtle Zinc/Warm Off-White (`#F4F4F5` / `#FBFBFC`) to cleanly establish physical separation behind raw lines.
- **Deep Line Neutral**: Solid Black (`#000000`) controls all containment outlines, divider rails, high-contrast labels, and offset drop planes.
- **Solar & Grid Active (Primary)**: Vibrant Electric Solar Green (`#16A34A` / `#22C55E`) delivers active energy states, generation surges, and net-positive exports. Dark Forest Green (`#166534`) grounds secondary green containers and contrast typography against green fills.
- **Energy Blue (Secondary)**: Grid Export Blue (`#0047FF`) indicates dynamic feed-in status, bidirectional transaction channels, and telemetry connections.
- **Terminal Alerts & Accents**: Alert Yellow (`#FFD600`) commands pending state transactions, peak load zones, and live sticker callouts. Error/Overload Red (`#FF3B30`) handles critical warnings, load shedding, and safety fault breakers.

## Typography

Typography acts as a structural instrument. The type pairing establishes clear boundaries between display authority and telemetry precision:

- **Display & Section Headers (Space Grotesk)**: Tightly tracked, ultra-bold, geometry-driven. Emits an engineered posture with idiosyncratic industrial cuts.
- **Operational Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono)**: Drives numerical metrics, energy currencies (kWh, EC, INR), system timestamps, status tickers, and tabular matrix data. Fixed-width character alignment ensures high-speed data stability without horizontal jitter during live streaming changes.
- **Narrative & System Interface (Inter)**: Clean, high-legibility sans-serif reserved strictly for explanatory copy, configuration descriptions, and form helper cues.
- **Metric Scalability**: Large numerical counters scale directly through the dedicated metric display tiers with tightly bound line heights to preserve visual weight against dense card containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a rigid 12-column modular grid with zero ambiguity:

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - Desktop (1200px+): 12 columns, 24px gutter, 40px outer canvas margin.
  - Tablet (768px - 1199px): 8 columns, 16px gutter, 24px canvas margin.
  - Mobile (< 767px): 4 columns, 12px gutter, 16px canvas margin.
- **Rhythm & Gaps**: Spacing relies on hard 4px base multiplier steps (`4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `40px`). Component interiors use tight, compact padding (`space-md` / 16px) to maximize data density per viewport fold.
- **Partition Lines**: Adjacent grid modules can share full-bleed 3px structural borders to form seamless tabular blocks, echoing industrial instrument consoles.

## Elevation & Depth

This system outright forbids gaussian blurs, soft drop shadows, diffused lighting, and translucent layered frosted glass. All elevation is 100% tactile, directional, and physical:

- **Hard Offset Shadow Matrix**: Elevation is expressed exclusively using a solid black `#000000` projection box with zero blur: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000`.
- **Active State Depress**: When an interactive element is activated (`:hover` / `:active`), the surface mechanically offsets down and right by 2px to 4px (`transform: translate(2px, 2px)` to `translate(4px, 4px)`) while reducing the box-shadow (`2px 2px 0px #000000` or `0px 0px 0px #000000`), perfectly mimicking a physical mechanical switch.
- **Structural Outlines**: All interactive containers, metric shields, and modals are enclosed in a mandatory 3px or 4px solid black border (`border: 3px solid #000000`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces an unapologetic `0` roundedness (sharp 90-degree corners) across standard interfaces. In specialized scenarios where ergonomics require softened corners (e.g. handheld mobile cards), a strictly bounded micro-radius of max 4px is permissible, but razor-sharp `0px` is the canonical default. 

Pills, organic bubbles, circular button housings, and soft blobs are strictly banned. Everything remains structural, architectural, and geometric.

## Components

### NeoButton
- **Construction**: 3px solid black border, sharp 0px corners, JetBrains Mono or Space Grotesk bold text, hard shadow (`4px 4px 0px #000000`).
- **Variants**:
  - *Primary Solar*: Background `#22C55E`, text `#000000`.
  - *Secondary Grid*: Background `#0047FF`, text `#FFFFFF`.
  - *Terminal Neutral*: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#000000`.
  - *Destructive Fault*: Background `#FF3B30`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Interaction**: On hover, shift `translate(-1px, -1px)` with shadow expanding to `5px 5px 0px #000000`. On active click, translate `translate(4px, 4px)` with shadow `0px 0px 0px #000000`.

### MetricCards
- **Construction**: Background `#FBFBFC` or `#FFFFFF`, 3px solid `#000000` border, `4px 4px 0px #000000` shadow.
- **Interior Structure**: Top status row with JetBrains Mono tracking label and sticker badge; central metric readout (e.g., `4,892.40 kWh`) using `metric-display` in Space Grotesk; bottom delta indicator framed by a crisp black divider rail.

### Sticker-Like Status Badges
- **Construction**: Inline-flex, 2px solid `#000000` border, `2px 2px 0px #000000` hard shadow, 0px border-radius, uppercase `label-mono-sm`.
- **Palette States**:
  - *LIVE*: Background `#22C55E`, text `#000000`.
  - *IDLE / SYNC*: Background `#FFD600`, text `#000000`.
  - *FAULT*: Background `#FF3B30`, text `#FFFFFF`.
  - *OFF-GRID*: Background `#F4F4F5`, text `#000000`.

### Solar & Grid Progress Bars
- **Frame**: 3px solid `#000000` track casing with `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F4F5` gutter fill, zero corner radius.
- **Active Bar Fill**: High-contrast fills (`#22C55E` or `#0047FF`). Striped segmented intervals marked by 2px black vertical separator ticks at 10% gradations to reinforce instrumentation precision.

### Live Ticker Tapes
- **Construction**: Full-bleed bar with top and bottom 3px solid `#000000` borders, background `#FFD600` or `#000000` with contrasting text.
- **Content**: JetBrains Mono ticker items scrolling continuously, separated by solid geometric symbols (`■`, `▲`, `▼`) marking instant changes in trade values, grid frequency (Hz), and localized node capacity.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp white or zinc surface, 3px solid `#000000` outline, JetBrains Mono typed input. On focus, add hard offset `3px 3px 0px #000000` without changing border color or using glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 3px solid black square box (even for radios; differentiate radios with an inner solid black square). Checked state displays a solid high-contrast `#22C55E` or `#000000` block fill.
